Career Scope in AI/ML

1. Data Scientist: Analyzes complex data sets, builds predictive models, and derives actionable insights.

2. Machine Learning Engineer: Designs, develops, and deploys ML models for various applications.

3. AI Researcher: Conducts advanced research in AI algorithms and explores emerging technologies.

4. Computer Vision Expert: Specializes in image and video analysis, facial recognition, and object detection.

5. Natural Language Processing (NLP) Specialist: Focuses on text and voice recognition, sentiment analysis, and language processing.
